## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Acme Pharma Ltd. selects, qualifies, approves, monitors and disqualifies suppliers of GMP materials and services. Only suppliers on the Approved Supplier List may supply materials or services that can affect product quality.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to suppliers of:

- starting materials and APIs;
- excipients;
- primary and printed packaging materials;
- contract manufacturing and contract testing laboratories; and
- GMP-critical services, such as calibration, pest control and computerised system hosting.

Suppliers of non-GMP goods, such as office supplies, are out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Purchasing | Places orders only with suppliers on the Approved Supplier List for the specific material and manufacturing site. |
| Supplier Quality Manager | Runs the qualification process, maintains the Approved Supplier List and schedules audits. |
| Quality Control | Tests qualification samples and incoming materials. |
| Head of QA | Approves suppliers in the High risk category and all disqualifications. |

## 4. Supplier risk categories

Each supplier is assigned a risk category based on the material's impact on product quality:

| Category | Examples | Audit requirement |
|---|---|---|
| High | API and starting material manufacturers, sterile primary packaging, contract manufacturers and contract laboratories | On-site audit before approval, then every **2 years** |
| Medium | Excipients, non-sterile primary packaging, printed packaging | Questionnaire before approval; on-site or remote audit every **3 years** |
| Low | Secondary packaging without printed text, GMP services with low impact | Questionnaire only; re-qualification every **3 years** |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Initial qualification

5.1.1 The Supplier Quality Manager sends the supplier a quality questionnaire covering the quality system, certifications, manufacturing sites, change notification practice and regulatory inspection history.

5.1.2 For High risk suppliers, an on-site audit is performed by a qualified Acme auditor before approval. A remote audit may replace the on-site audit only with Head of QA approval and a documented justification.

5.1.3 QC tests at least **three qualification lots** of the material against the full specification before first commercial use. For High risk materials, one of the three lots is also used in a trial batch.

5.1.4 A written quality agreement is signed before the first commercial delivery for all High and Medium risk suppliers. The agreement defines responsibilities for change notification, deviations, complaints, audits and retention of records.

5.1.5 After satisfactory results, the supplier is added to the Approved Supplier List for the specific material and manufacturing site. Approval is site-specific: a different manufacturing site of the same company requires separate qualification.

### 5.2 Incoming material testing

5.2.1 Every incoming lot is sampled and at least one identity test is performed.

5.2.2 For approved suppliers with a good performance record, QC may rely on the supplier's certificate of analysis for other tests. The supplier's certificate results are verified by full testing of at least **one lot per year**, or every **10th lot**, whichever is more frequent.

### 5.3 Performance monitoring

5.3.1 The Supplier Quality Manager evaluates each High and Medium risk supplier **annually** using a scorecard with these weighted criteria: rejected lots (40%), deviations and complaints attributable to the supplier (30%), on-time delivery (15%), and responsiveness to quality requests (15%).

5.3.2 A score below **70 out of 100** places the supplier on probation. A corrective action plan is requested and an audit may be brought forward.

5.3.3 Supplier performance results are reported in the Product Quality Review (SOP-QA-015).

### 5.4 Change notification

Suppliers must notify Acme of any change in manufacturing site, process, specification or sub-supplier before implementation, as defined in the quality agreement. Acme assesses the change under SOP-QA-012.

### 5.5 Disqualification

5.5.1 A supplier is disqualified when:

- it remains on probation for more than **12 months** without improvement;
- a critical audit finding is not corrected; or
- it fails to notify Acme of a significant change.

5.5.2 Disqualification is approved by the Head of QA. The supplier is removed from the Approved Supplier List and Purchasing blocks further orders.

## 6. Records

Qualification files, audit reports, quality agreements and scorecards are kept for as long as the supplier is approved, plus **6 years**.
