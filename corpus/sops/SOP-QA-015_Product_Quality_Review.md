## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Acme Pharma Ltd. performs the Product Quality Review (PQR): a regular, documented review of each product to verify the consistency of the manufacturing process, the appropriateness of current specifications, and to identify trends and opportunities for improvement.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to every commercial API and finished product manufactured or packaged at the site, including products made for other companies under contract. Products in development and clinical trial materials are excluded.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| PQR coordinator (QA) | Plans the annual PQR schedule, compiles the report and tracks actions. |
| Production | Provides yields, process data, batch counts and deviations. |
| Quality Control | Provides test results, OOS and OOT data and stability results. |
| Regulatory Affairs | Provides the status of regulatory filings and commitments. |
| Engineering | Provides the qualification status of equipment and utilities. |
| Head of QA | Approves the PQR report and its conclusions. |

## 4. Frequency and review period

4.1 A PQR is performed for each product **annually**. The review period is 12 consecutive months, aligned to the calendar year.

4.2 The PQR report must be approved within **90 calendar days** after the end of the review period.

4.3 Where fewer than **three batches** were manufactured in the review period, the PQR still covers all batches made. QA may extend the review to the previous period to allow meaningful trending, and documents this decision.

4.4 Products not manufactured during the review period receive a short PQR stating that no batches were made, together with the status of stability, complaints and regulatory commitments.

## 5. Content of the PQR

### 5.1 Manufacturing and process data

The PQR reviews:

- the number of batches manufactured, released, rejected and reprocessed;
- process yields, with statistical trending of critical process parameters and in-process controls;
- a review of all critical deviations and investigations, and a trend of all deviations by root-cause category (SOP-QA-005);
- all changes to processes, methods and materials (SOP-QA-012).

### 5.2 Quality control data

The PQR reviews:

- critical quality attribute results, trended using control charts, and the process capability index (Cpk) for key attributes. A Cpk below **1.33** triggers an assessment of process robustness;
- all OOS and OOT results and their investigations (SOP-QC-007);
- the stability programme results and any adverse trends.

### 5.3 Quality system data

The PQR reviews:

- product complaints, returns and recalls;
- the status and effectiveness of CAPAs (SOP-QA-008);
- the results of supplier performance reviews for the starting materials (SOP-QA-019);
- regulatory commitments, variations and inspection findings;
- the qualification status of relevant equipment and utilities; and
- the continued process verification data under SOP-VA-002.

### 5.4 Previous PQR

The PQR checks whether the actions from the previous PQR have been completed and were effective.

## 6. Conclusions and actions

6.1 The PQR concludes whether the process remains in a state of control, whether specifications remain appropriate, and whether revalidation is required.

6.2 Each recommended action is raised as a CAPA under SOP-QA-008, or as a change control under SOP-QA-012, with an owner and due date.

6.3 A PQR conclusion that the process is **not** in a state of control is escalated to the Head of QA and the Site Director, and is presented at the next management review.

## 7. Management review

PQR summaries for all products are presented at the quarterly management review of process performance and product quality. Management confirms that resources are available for the actions raised.

## 8. Records

Approved PQR reports and their supporting data are kept for the life of the product plus **6 years**.
