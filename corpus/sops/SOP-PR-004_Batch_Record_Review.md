## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how executed batch production records and packaging records are reviewed by Production and Quality Assurance before a batch is released. It ensures that each batch was made according to the approved master record, that all deviations are investigated, and that the batch documentation is complete and accurate.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all batches of APIs, intermediates and finished products manufactured or packaged at the site, including electronic batch records (EBR) and paper batch records. Final batch certification by the authorised person is described in the release procedure and is not covered here; this SOP covers the review that supports it.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Production operator | Records each step at the time it is performed and signs for the work done. |
| Production supervisor | Performs the first-line review of the batch record within 2 working days of batch completion. |
| QA batch record reviewer | Performs the independent QA review and compiles the batch release package. |
| QC | Provides approved analytical results and the certificate of analysis. |
| Authorised person / QA release | Decides on batch release using the release package. |

## 4. Definitions

- **Master batch record (MBR):** the approved instruction for manufacturing a product, controlled under SOP-QA-001.
- **Executed batch record:** the completed copy of the MBR for one batch, including all attached printouts.
- **Release package:** the executed batch record, the QC results, deviation and change control status, and the review checklist.
- **Review by exception:** for EBR systems, a review focused on entries flagged by the system as exceptions, such as limits exceeded, manual overrides or alarms.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Production review

5.1.1 The production supervisor reviews the executed batch record within **2 working days** after batch completion.

5.1.2 The supervisor checks that every step is completed and signed, that all in-process control results are within limits, that yields are within the stated range, and that equipment and line clearance records are attached.

5.1.3 Any departure found during the review is raised as a deviation under SOP-QA-005 before the record is passed to QA.

### 5.2 QA review

5.2.1 The QA reviewer completes the review within **5 working days** after receiving the record from Production.

5.2.2 The QA review uses checklist PR-004-F2 and covers:

- completeness of all entries, signatures and dates;
- critical process parameters and in-process controls against the limits in the MBR;
- yield reconciliation: the actual yield must be within **95–102%** of the theoretical yield unless the MBR states another range. Results outside the range require a deviation;
- label and packaging material reconciliation. Any discrepancy in printed packaging material counts requires a deviation before release;
- correct use of good documentation practice (SOP-QA-001, section 6);
- the status of all related deviations, OOS investigations and change controls; and
- for EBR, the exception report and the batch audit trail (SOP-IT-003).

5.2.3 Review comments are returned to Production. Corrections follow good documentation practice and are reviewed again by QA.

### 5.3 Release readiness

5.3.1 A batch is ready for the release decision only when:

- the production and QA reviews are complete;
- all QC results are approved and within specification;
- all critical deviations related to the batch are closed, and major deviations have a documented QA impact assessment that allows release;
- there is no open OOS investigation for the batch (SOP-QC-007); and
- for the first batch after a major or critical change, all pre-implementation actions of the change control are complete (SOP-QA-012).

5.3.2 The QA reviewer compiles the release package and forwards it to the authorised person.

### 5.4 Review by exception for EBR

5.4.1 For batches made using the validated EBR system, QA may use review by exception. Steps that the system has verified against limits do not need line-by-line review.

5.4.2 All exceptions, manual entries, overrides and alarms must be reviewed. The EBR configuration must be validated to show that exceptions are reliably flagged (SOP-VA-002, SOP-IT-003).

## 6. Timeliness metric

The target is to complete the full batch record review, Production and QA, within **10 working days** of batch completion for at least **90% of batches**. Performance is reported monthly.

## 7. Records

Executed batch records and release packages are kept for **batch expiry plus 1 year, and no less than 6 years**.
