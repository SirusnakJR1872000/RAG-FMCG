## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the corrective action and preventive action (CAPA) system of Acme Pharma Ltd. It describes how CAPAs are initiated, planned, implemented, verified for effectiveness and closed, so that the causes of quality problems are removed and their recurrence is prevented.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to CAPAs arising from:

- deviations (SOP-QA-005);
- out-of-specification investigations (SOP-QC-007);
- customer complaints and product recalls;
- internal audits, supplier audits and regulatory inspections;
- Product Quality Reviews (SOP-QA-015) and management review;
- audit trail review findings (SOP-IT-003); and
- trend analysis of any quality data.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CAPA owner | Plans and implements the actions and provides objective evidence of completion. |
| Department head | Ensures resources are available and approves the CAPA plan for their area. |
| Quality Assurance | Approves the CAPA plan, verifies implementation, performs or approves the effectiveness check and closes the CAPA. |
| CAPA Review Board | Chaired by the Head of QA. Meets monthly to review overdue CAPAs, extensions and effectiveness failures. |

## 4. Definitions

- **Corrective action:** action to eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity and prevent its recurrence.
- **Preventive action:** action to eliminate the cause of a potential nonconformity and prevent its occurrence.
- **Correction:** immediate action to fix the nonconformity itself, for example reworking a label. A correction alone is not a CAPA.
- **Effectiveness check:** a planned, documented verification that the CAPA achieved its objective and that the problem has not recurred.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 CAPA initiation criteria

5.1.1 QA shall initiate a CAPA when an investigation identifies a **confirmed assignable cause**, a **recurrence** of a previously seen problem, or a **systemic quality risk**.

5.1.2 A CAPA is mandatory for:

- every critical or major deviation;
- every OOS investigation that confirms a laboratory or process cause, or where the same OOS has occurred more than once in 12 months (see SOP-QC-007, section 5.6);
- every major or critical audit or inspection observation; and
- every confirmed product complaint related to quality.

5.1.3 A CAPA is recorded in the eQMS within **5 working days** of the triggering record being approved. It is linked to that record.

### 5.2 CAPA plan

5.2.1 The CAPA owner writes a plan that states: the problem statement, the root cause it addresses, each action with an owner and due date, the evidence expected, and the effectiveness check criteria.

5.2.2 Actions are prioritised by risk using the Acme risk matrix (severity × likelihood, scored 1–5 each):

| Risk score | Priority | Target implementation time |
|---|---|---|
| 15–25 | High | 30 calendar days |
| 8–14 | Medium | 90 calendar days |
| 1–7 | Low | 180 calendar days |

5.2.3 Where an action changes a validated process, system, facility or controlled document, the change is made through change control under SOP-QA-012. The change control number is recorded in the CAPA.

### 5.3 Implementation and extensions

5.3.1 The CAPA owner implements the actions and attaches objective evidence, such as revised documents, training records or validation reports.

5.3.2 An extension of a due date requires a justification and an interim risk assessment. The first extension is approved by QA. Any second extension is approved by the CAPA Review Board. No CAPA may be extended more than twice.

### 5.4 Effectiveness check

5.4.1 Every CAPA with High or Medium priority has an effectiveness check. For Low priority CAPAs, QA decides whether an effectiveness check is needed and documents the reason.

5.4.2 The effectiveness check is performed **90 calendar days** after implementation, or after a defined number of batches stated in the plan, typically the next **three consecutive batches**.

5.4.3 The check is passed when the defined criteria are met, for example no recurrence of the deviation type during the monitoring period.

5.4.4 If the effectiveness check fails, the CAPA is not closed. A new investigation is opened and the failure is reported to the CAPA Review Board.

### 5.5 Closure

5.5.1 QA closes the CAPA when all actions are complete with evidence and the effectiveness check is passed or justified as not required.

5.5.2 Closed CAPAs cannot be edited. Any further action is raised as a new CAPA linked to the original.

## 6. Metrics and management review

6.1 QA reports these CAPA metrics monthly: number open, number overdue, average age, extensions granted and effectiveness-check pass rate.

6.2 A summary is presented at the quarterly management review of process performance and product quality. The review decides on resources and escalations.

6.3 Acme's target is **fewer than 5% of CAPAs overdue** at any month end.

## 7. Records

CAPA records, plans, evidence and effectiveness checks are kept in the eQMS for at least **6 years** after closure.
