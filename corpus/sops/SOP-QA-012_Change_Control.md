## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how changes that could affect product quality, validated status or regulatory compliance are proposed, assessed, approved, implemented and verified at Acme Pharma Ltd. It ensures that no such change is made without a documented evaluation of its impact.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to permanent changes to:

- manufacturing processes, batch sizes and process parameters;
- raw materials, packaging materials and their suppliers;
- specifications and analytical methods;
- facilities, equipment and utilities;
- computerised systems, including software versions, configuration and user roles that affect GMP data;
- GMP documents such as SOPs, master batch records and specifications; and
- regulatory filings.

Temporary, one-off departures are handled as planned deviations under SOP-QA-005. Emergency repairs on a like-for-like basis are handled by the maintenance procedure, but any repair that is not like-for-like requires change control.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Change initiator | Raises the change request and describes the change and its reason. |
| Change owner | Coordinates the impact assessment, the action plan and implementation. |
| Subject matter experts | Assess the impact in their area: Production, QC, Engineering, Validation, Regulatory Affairs, IT. |
| Change Control Board (CCB) | Reviews and approves major and critical changes. Meets weekly; chaired by QA. |
| Quality Assurance | Classifies the change, approves the plan, approves release of the first batch after the change, and closes the record. |

## 4. Definitions

- **Change request:** the formal proposal for a change, recorded in the eQMS with a number in the format CC-YYYY-NNNN.
- **Like-for-like:** a replacement with identical make, model, material and specification, requiring no change to any document.
- **Effectiveness review:** a check after implementation that the change achieved its objective with no unintended effects.

## 5. Classification

| Class | Definition | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | No impact on product quality, validated state or regulatory filing. For example, editorial corrections to an SOP. | QA |
| Major | Possible impact on product quality or validated state, with no regulatory filing change. For example, a new equipment model, or a change to a non-critical process parameter. | Change Control Board |
| Critical | Impact on product quality or a regulatory filing. For example, a change of API supplier, a new manufacturing site, or a change to a critical process parameter. | Change Control Board and Head of QA; Regulatory Affairs confirms the filing strategy |

QA classifies each change request within **5 working days** of submission.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Raising a change request

6.1.1 The initiator records the change request in the eQMS. It describes the current state, the proposed state, the reason for the change and the affected products, systems and documents.

6.1.2 No part of the change may be implemented before the change request is approved. Purchasing equipment or materials for evaluation is allowed but must be noted in the request.

### 6.2 Impact assessment

6.2.1 Each relevant subject matter expert documents an impact assessment covering: product quality; validation and qualification status; stability; regulatory filings; documentation and training; computerised systems and data integrity; supply; and health, safety and environment.

6.2.2 The risk of the change is assessed using the principles of ICH Q9(R1). Where the risk score is 15 or above on the Acme risk matrix (see SOP-QA-008), a formal risk assessment such as an FMEA is attached.

6.2.3 For computerised systems, the impact assessment states whether revalidation is needed and whether the audit trail configuration is affected (see SOP-IT-003).

### 6.3 Approval and action plan

6.3.1 The change owner writes an action plan listing each pre-implementation and post-implementation action with an owner and due date: for example document revision, training, validation, stability studies and regulatory submissions.

6.3.2 Critical changes affecting a regulatory filing are not implemented until Regulatory Affairs confirms that the required approval or notification is in place.

### 6.4 Implementation

6.4.1 Actions are completed and evidence is attached to the record.

6.4.2 The first batch manufactured after a major or critical change is released only after QA confirms that all pre-implementation actions are complete. This batch is flagged in the batch record review under SOP-PR-004.

### 6.5 Effectiveness review and closure

6.5.1 For major and critical changes, an effectiveness review is performed after the first **three batches** or **6 months**, whichever comes first.

6.5.2 QA closes the change record when all actions are complete and the effectiveness review is acceptable. The target is to close a change within **180 days** of approval.

6.5.3 If the effectiveness review identifies unintended effects, a deviation is raised under SOP-QA-005.

## 7. Records

Change records, impact assessments and evidence are kept in the eQMS for the life of the product plus **6 years**.
